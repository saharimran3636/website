Introduction of Pakistan 🇵🇰

Pakistan is a beautiful country located in South Asia. It became independent on 14th August 1947. The founder of Pakistan was Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah. Islamabad is the capital of Pakistan. Urdu is our national language. Pakistan is famous for its beautiful mountains, rivers, and rich culture.

hunza valleys

Hunza Valley is one of the most beautiful places in Pakistan. It is located in Gilgit-Baltistan, in the northern part of the country It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, clear rivers, beautiful lakes, and green valleys.

Location: Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan Famous Mountains: Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar Famous Lake: Attabad Lake Historical Places: Baltit Fort and Altit Fort Local Culture: Rich traditions and warm hospitality Best Feature: Stunning natural beauty and peaceful surroundings

Mazar-e-Quaid

Mazar-e-Quaid one of the most famous historical landmarks in Pakistan . It is located in Karachi . It is the final resting place of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan. It was completed in 1971 and is famous for its beautiful white marble building and impressive dome. Mazar-e-Quaid is a symbol of Pakistan’s independence, history, and national pride. 🇵🇰
